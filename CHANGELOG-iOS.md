# What's new in Firn for iPhone and iPad

## Firn 1.0.0

**Firn, now on iPhone and iPad.**

### New

- **Every space is its own browser,** with its own tabs, sign-ins, and history.
- **Basecamp.** Your everyday sites, one tap away.
- **Groups.** Drag one tab onto another. They name themselves, on your device.
- **One bar.** Search, go to a site, or jump to a tab.
- **Reader.** Just the article.
- **Summarize.** The article in a few sentences, made on your device.
- **Ads and trackers are blocked** from the start.
- **iCloud sync** keeps your spaces the same on your devices. It's off until you turn it on in Settings.

Firn needs iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Summarize and group names need Apple Intelligence.
