# What's new in Firn

## Firn 1.0.1

### Fixed

- **Full screen looks like the rest of Firn.** The blank bar across the top is gone. The window buttons stay where they always are, and when the menu bar comes down, the sidebar and the top of the page glide down with it. Thanks to u/Disastrous-Job-9948 on Reddit for reporting it.
- **The page's corners match the window's** on macOS Tahoe. Thanks to Noah for reporting it.

## Firn 1.0.0

**Firn, made for the Mac.**

Firn is now a native Mac app, built from scratch. It's quicker and lighter, and it feels at home on your Mac. Each space is now its own browser, with its own sign-ins, so Work and Personal never mix.

**A fresh start.** Your tabs, spaces, and passwords from the earlier Firn don't come over to this one. They stay on your Mac, untouched.

### New

- **Every space is truly separate.** Each space has its own Basecamp, tabs, sign-ins, cookies, and history. Signing in to Gmail in Work doesn't sign you in to Personal. When you make a space, you can choose to share sign-ins with another one instead.
- **Groups** keep the tabs you want to hold on to. Drag one tab onto another to start a group.
- **Tidied away.** Tabs you haven't looked at for a while drift into a small box at the bottom of the sidebar, so you can see where they went and bring them back.
- **Lookout, split view, and the command bar.** Glance at a link before you keep it, put two pages side by side, and press ⌘T to type an address, search, or jump to a tab.
- **Saved passwords**, kept in your Mac's Keychain. Bring yours over from another browser or password manager.
- **Coming from Arc?** Firn can bring over your spaces, favorites, and pinned tabs.
- **Ads and trackers are blocked** on every page, and you can turn it off for a site that doesn't work.
- **Firn keeps itself up to date.** It checks for new versions, and you can turn that off in Settings.
