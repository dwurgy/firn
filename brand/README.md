# Firn brand

The logo and app icons, made with Claude Design. The mark is a firn crystal:
the small, settled, six-armed star of compacted alpine snow, with soft
rounded arms.

- `svg/` – the mark, wordmark ("firn") and lockups, for light, dark and
  Glacier backgrounds; app icon artwork in Glacier, Sand and Dark.
- `app-icons/` – ready-made app icons (Windows `.ico`, macOS `.icns`, PNGs).
- `favicon/` – favicons (16–512px, `.ico`, `.svg`).

Firn itself uses the **Glacier** app icon (`../assets/icon.*`) and the mark
on its "No open tabs" page.

Colors: Glacier `#6E98B2` (mark) on frame sand `#E9E3DA`, page white
`#FBFAF8`, ink `#241F1B`, dark frame `#3A3734`.
