**Description**

> Firn is a calm browser for iPhone and iPad. The page is the star, and everything else stays quietly out of the way.
>
> EVERY SPACE IS ITS OWN BROWSER
> Keep Work and Personal truly apart. Each space has its own tabs, sign-ins, cookies, and history, so signing in to Gmail in one doesn't sign you in to the other. Or share sign-ins between spaces when you make one.
>
> BASECAMP
> Your everyday sites, one tap away at the top of each space.
>
> GROUPS
> Drag one tab onto another to keep them together. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Firn names the group for you.
>
> ONE BAR FOR EVERYTHING
> Type an address, search, jump to an open tab, or find an action by name.
>
> READ WITHOUT THE CLUTTER
> Reader shows just the article. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Firn can sum it up in a few calm sentences, made on your device.
>
> PRIVATE FROM THE START
> Ads and trackers are blocked with lists built into the app. There's no account, no analytics, and no tracking.
>
> WITH YOUR OTHER DEVICES
> Turn on iCloud sync to keep your spaces the same everywhere, find your open tabs, and send a tab to another device. It's off until you turn it on, and encrypted on your device first.
>
> Firn is open source, and made by one person. If it makes your day a little calmer, there's a tip jar in Settings.

**Review notes**

> Firn is a web browser built on WebKit. No account or sign-in is needed.
>
> Tips: open the tabs view (the two squares at the bottom right), tap the space's name at the bottom, then Settings, and scroll to Tip Jar. Tips are consumable and unlock nothing.
>
> Privacy policy: in the same Settings screen, under Privacy.
>
> Default browser: Firn has the com.apple.developer.web-browser entitlement, granted by Apple. Settings > Default Browser opens Firn's page in the Settings app.
>
> iCloud sync is optional and off by default. Summaries and automatic group names need Apple Intelligence.
>
> Camera and microphone are used only when a website asks and the person allows it. Photos access is add-only, for Save to Photos on a web page's picture.
