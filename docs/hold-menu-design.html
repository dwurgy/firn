The hold menu

Hold a tab, a group's name, or a Basecamp tile still for half a second. The tile lifts on its solid base, everything else dims softly, and the menu forms beside it like frost. Each scene is shown in light and dark.

The menus work: tap Move to Group to see its list glide into the same card.

Close rows

1. A slim row

Low in the list, so the menu opens above it. It lines up with the row's left side, where the finger usually is.

2. A tall card

The same menu, below the card because there's room. The card keeps its picture while lifted.

3. A group

Holding the name lifts the whole group. A short menu.

4. A Basecamp tile

A tile on the right, so the menu lines up with its right edge and stays inside the screen.

5. Move to Group's list

The same card, glided to a new height, with a back row at the top. Never a second card.
