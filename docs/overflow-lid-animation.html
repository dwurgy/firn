The snow box lid

Two drafts of the tidy moment. Site icons drift down into the overflow button, the lid lifts to catch them, then settles shut before the pill says what happened. Both end on the approved D3 shape.

The lid tips open about 20° on its right corner as the first icon nears, stays open while the rest fall in, and settles shut as the last one lands. No bounce.

The same lid. A beat after it tips, the drift slumps downhill toward the hinge as one soft lump, slow to start and stopping short. As the lid lands, the jolt settles it back into its usual shape.

Timing follows the app: icons leave 90ms apart and take 0.7s to fall, the frost forms in 0.25s, the pill holds for 2.6s. Click the button after it plays to clear the dot.
