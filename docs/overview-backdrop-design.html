Overview backdrop

Safari's tab overview sits on your wallpaper, blurred hard, and that light and shadow are what make its cards float. Firn can't read the wallpaper, so this tries Sunrise's horizon band (no sky), blurred, and printed in one color: the space's own. The depth comes from Sunrise; the color is the space's alone, with no sky blue.

Round two (2026-11-18): the one-color version, as built, feels bland, since one hue however shaded stays monochrome, while Safari's wallpaper blurs two or three hues into each other. Three ways to bring in more color while the space's color stays in charge: Dawn glow (the space's color in the shadows, Sunrise's warm apricot in the highlights), Neighbor hues (two soft patches of the space color's neighbors on the color wheel), and Tab colors (soft patches from the open pages' own colors, so the backdrop changes with what's open). Vivid sets how strongly the extra color comes through.
