# firnbrowser.com/privacy: what it needs for iPhone and iPad

Compared with the live page and the iPhone's code on 2026-11-25, before submitting iPhone and iPad 1.0. The page is the website's (`dwurgy/firn-site`), changed in its own session; this is the list and the words to bring there. Post the iCloud words before the app goes live: the page promises to say so first. The app's Settings › Privacy › Privacy Policy opens that page.

## Wrong once the iPhone and iPad ship

1. **"A calm browser for the Mac" and "What you do in Firn stays on your Mac":** these need to cover all three devices.
2. **iCloud sync isn't mentioned.** It's off by default, but "None of it is uploaded anywhere" is false once it's on. The Mac needs this too, once a version with sync is out.
3. **Backups aren't mentioned.** Tabs and history are in an iPhone's own backups, iCloud Backup included. Page pictures are left out.
4. **Crash reports:** Firn sends none, but Apple can share them with the developer if someone opts in.

## Mac only, so label it that way

Update checks, saved passwords, the Arc import, ⇧⌘T, the View menu (the iPhone's Stop Blocking is in the page menu), the `~/Library/Containers/` path, the Downloads folder, the sandbox list, and tabs tidying away (the iPhone doesn't tidy).

## Missing

5. **History:** kept for a year on each device, and it can be cleared. The Mac's page doesn't say this either.
6. **Page pictures:** the tab overview saves a picture of each page on the device.
7. **Downloads:** On My iPhone › Firn › Downloads.
8. **Photos:** add-only, after iOS asks.
9. **Tips:** Apple handles payment.
10. **Send Feedback:** the person's own mail app sends it, with Firn's version, the iOS version, and the device model filled in.
11. **Summaries and group names:** Apple's on-device model.

## Don't promise on the iPhone

- **Camera and microphone:** Firn's own question, with its https-only and "only while that tab is open" rules, is the Mac's. On the iPhone, iOS asks instead.
- **Scam checks:** Firn leaves WebKit's default in place, but no test of it on the iPhone was found. Try a test scam page on a real iPhone before the page says so.

## Words to paste

> Firn is a calm browser for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. What you do in Firn stays on your device, unless you turn on iCloud sync or back up your device.

> **iCloud sync, only if you turn it on.** It's off by default. When it's on, your spaces, your open tabs' addresses and titles, and any tab you send to another device go to your own iCloud. They're encrypted on your device first, so only your devices can read them. Groups, Basecamp, history, settings, sign-ins, and passwords don't sync. Turning sync off asks iCloud to remove that device's list of open tabs.

> **History** is kept on your device for a year, separately for each space unless spaces share sign-ins. You can clear it any time.

> **On iPhone and iPad,** Firn also saves a picture of each open page for the tab overview. Pictures are deleted with their tab and left out of backups. Your tabs, history, and settings can be part of your device's own backup, including iCloud Backup if you use it. Downloads are saved in Firn's folder in the Files app. If you save a picture to Photos, iOS asks first, and Firn can only add to your library, never look through it.

> **Camera and microphone on iPhone and iPad.** iOS asks before a site can use either.

> **Summaries and group names** are made by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent anywhere.

> **Tips** are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details.

> **Feedback** opens a draft in your mail app with Firn's version, your iOS version, and your device model. Nothing is sent until you send it.

> **Crash reports.** Firn sends none. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share crash reports with me.
