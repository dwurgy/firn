Reader: the look

A draft for step 1. The page becomes warm paper and one quiet column. The only choices are Serif or Sans, and text size, in one small Aa button. The space's color shows in three places: the book in the address bar when Reader is on, the link underlines, and a quote's thin line. Click Aa to open its popover, and the book to see it off and on.
