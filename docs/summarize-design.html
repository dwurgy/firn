Summarize: the look

A draft for step 5's design. Reader's look doesn't change; Summarize adds one quiet pill to the article's heading, and a soft card it grows into. Everything is made on the device by Apple Intelligence; on a Mac or iPhone without it, none of this appears. Pick a state below to see each moment, or click the pill and the card's ✕ in the mockups.

  • The suggestion: every time Reader opens, a small "Summarize" pill sits under the byline, inside the article's heading. Sans, muted, on the warm wash, like the byline's quieter sibling. No color, no sparkle, no motion.
  • Being made: the pill grows into the card (0.26s, bounce-free). Three soft lines with one slow sheen stand in for the sentences; it stops the moment they arrive. No spinner, no percent.
  • The summary: a few calm sentences (two to four), in the reader's own font (Serif or Sans follow the Aa choice), a touch smaller than the article. They settle in from the top down once, a soft edge sliding down the paragraph, then hold still. A last line says where it came from: "Made on this iPhone by Apple Intelligence. It can make mistakes."
  • Going away: the ✕ folds the card back into the pill. Opening it again shows the same summary at once (kept only while Reader is open; never saved).
  • Couldn't: when the model declines (an unsupported language, or a topic it won't touch), the card says so in one plain line, then folds back to the pill after a few seconds. Nothing in red.

On the iPhone: the way in

As Safari: on a page that reads as an article, the page menu (the book, or holding the field) gets Summarize right under Show Reader. Picking it opens Reader with the card already being made at the top. Tapping the book while "Reader available" shows goes straight into Reader (no menu), and there the pill waits under the byline.

On the Mac

The same pill and card in the reader. Other ways in: View › Summarize Article (just under Show Reader, no shortcut of its own) and Summarize in the command bar, both only on articles, and both open Reader first, as on the iPhone.
